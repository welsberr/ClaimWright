# Academic Publication Integrity Sources

Last reviewed: 2026-07-31

ClaimWright's academic-publication integrity gate uses the following primary
guidance as a baseline. Destination journals, institutions, funders, and laws
may impose additional or stricter requirements.

## Research Misconduct

The U.S. Office of Research Integrity defines research misconduct as
fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism in proposing, performing, reviewing,
or reporting research. It distinguishes misconduct from honest error and
differences of opinion.

- https://ori.hhs.gov/definition-research-misconduct

## Text Recycling And Overlapping Publication

The Committee on Publication Ethics distinguishes unattributed use of another
author's work from text recycling in an author's own publications. Its guidance
calls for case-specific review of the amount, location, acknowledgment,
copyright status, and context of overlap.

- https://members.publicationethics.org/sites/default/files/Web_A29298_COPE_Text_Recycling.pdf

The International Committee of Medical Journal Editors requires transparent
handling of duplicate submission, prior publication, preprints, secondary
publication, and manuscripts using the same data.

- https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/publishing-and-editorial-issues/overlapping-publications.html

## AI-Assisted Work, Authorship, And Confidentiality

ICMJE assigns responsibility for accuracy, integrity, originality, attribution,
and disclosure to human authors. AI systems must not be authors. AI use should
be disclosed as required, and confidential manuscripts must not be uploaded to
systems that cannot preserve confidentiality without explicit authority.

- https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/artificial-intelligence/
- https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/defining-the-role-of-authors-and-contributors.html

## ClaimWright Interpretation

A similarity report is a discovery aid, not a finding of plagiarism and not a
certificate of originality. Coverage gaps, inaccessible literature,
cross-language overlap, idea or data appropriation, image reuse, and
misattributed paraphrase can all escape a raw text score. ClaimWright therefore
requires a documented human disposition of material matches plus the broader
integrity review in `policies/academic_publication.yaml`.
