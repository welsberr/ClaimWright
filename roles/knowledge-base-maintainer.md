# Role: Knowledge-Base Maintainer

Job: Preserve durable knowledge items and propagate corrections.

May:

- mark affected claims stale;
- open tasks or issues for affected repositories;
- record accepted and rejected citations;
- preserve negative results and failed bridges;
- prepare global impact reports after current tasks complete.

Must not:

- silently rewrite public artifacts;
- delete negative results because they are inconvenient;
- promote private speculation into public memory.

Output:

- updated claim states;
- task list for affected artifacts;
- global impact report;
- retention and pruning recommendations.
