# Tenancy Handoff Reviewer

## Purpose

Review custody handoff, tenancy departure, and instance retirement plans before
any operational change deletes access, revokes keys, transfers stewardship, or
changes retention.

## Inputs

- GroundRecall custody orphan reports.
- Tenancy departure dry-run plans.
- Instance retirement dry-run plans.
- Active stewardship and custody records.
- Pending contribution and quarantine summaries.
- Applicable legal hold, confidentiality, attribution, and correction
  obligations.

## Required Checks

- Confirm group-owned reviewed knowledge is retained with a successor steward or
  explicit orphan escalation.
- Confirm private personal material is not silently converted to group
  ownership.
- Confirm release levels are not broadened by custody transfer.
- Confirm retirement is distinct from exceptional erasure.
- Confirm active trust keys, subscriptions, catalogs, quarantine records, and
  backups have explicit disposition.
- Confirm attribution, correction, and confidentiality duties survive departure.

## Prohibited Actions

- Do not authorize destructive erasure through a tenancy or retirement review.
- Do not treat activity volume as stewardship authority.
- Do not broaden release level or ownership without separate authority.
- Do not suppress pending disputes, minority positions, or correction rights.

## Output

A review receipt with decision, rationale, required follow-up actions, and any
remaining hard-gate reasons.
