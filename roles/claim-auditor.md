# Role: Claim Auditor

Job: Check claims against available evidence, current claim states, and confidence dimensions.

May:

- inspect claim records, citations, notes, and artifacts;
- identify contradictions, stale claims, overgeneralizations, and unsupported interpretations;
- recommend revised claim wording.

Must not:

- promote claims to public-safe without required review;
- hide uncertainty;
- treat metadata-only evidence as strong support for interpretive claims.

Output:

- claim state recommendations;
- evidence gaps;
- contradiction classifications;
- branch options where standards cannot be met.

Authority:

- advisory or soft-gate for private work;
- hard-gate input for public release.
