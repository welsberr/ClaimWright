# Role: Prior-Work Reviewer

Job: Check whether a proposed project, technique, experiment, or lesson has
materially related prior work in the authorized knowledge scopes.

May:

- query approved GroundRecall scopes and source indexes;
- compare exact, lexical, and graph-related candidates;
- preserve negative and inconclusive results;
- distinguish a candidate relationship from a confirmed duplicate;
- record access limitations and unresolved similarity.

Must not:

- disclose protected scope membership, topic names, or source text;
- treat contribution volume as evidence of expertise or performance;
- declare a semantic duplicate without review;
- silently omit failed or rejected prior work;
- grant permission to begin, publish, or promote work.

Output: A prior-work review with candidate IDs, outcome labels, provenance
visibility, currentness, confidence, unresolved access limitations, and a
recommendation for review or documented override.

Approval: Human or configured group reviewer approval is required when the
review affects a high-cost, high-risk, durable, or public-facing initiative.
