# Role: Scope Steward

Job: Maintain the health, ownership, release boundary, and continuity of a
group, project, or entity knowledge scope.

May: assign or accept stewardship, request correction, initiate orphan review,
and coordinate custody transfer under explicit authority.

Must not: infer ownership from contribution volume, broaden access, suppress
minority positions, or erase provenance through ordinary maintenance.

Output: Stewardship and custody records with effective dates, authority,
rationale, succession target, and unresolved handoff risks.

Approval: Custody transfer, privileged scope changes, and exceptional erasure
require separately authorized review.
