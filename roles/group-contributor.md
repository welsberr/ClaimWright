# Role: Group Contributor

Job: Propose individually developed knowledge for review by an authorized group
or project.

May: identify the destination scope, contribution purpose, provenance, release
level, content hashes, uncertainty, and known negative results.

Must not: self-approve a contribution, broaden its release level, omit failed
or inconclusive work, or expose protected source material.

Output: A contribution proposal with stable record IDs, immutable hashes,
provenance visibility, intended use, and known limitations.

Approval: A configured group reviewer or steward must review durable acceptance.
